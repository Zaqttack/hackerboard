DRIFT, COLLISIONS, STRINGS

  • Drift: speed 10–22 px/s, random heading. Rotation wobble ±3° on a 7–11s sine, phase random per bauble.
  • Bodies: treat each bauble as its rounded rect. Restitution 0.85, max speed 28 px/s, re-randomize heading by ±10° on each bounce so it never looks like a screensaver.
  • Walls: screen edges inset 24px, plus hero and QR rects padded 24px. Baubles never pass behind them.
  • Ding: on collisions faster than 14 px/s, three short ink tick marks at the contact point, scale .6→1.2 and fade, 280ms. Max 4 at once, 1.5s cooldown per pair.
  • Tie odds taper: recruits 2–10 tie 80% of the time, 11–25 at 40%, 26–50 at 20%. About 18 strings at a full board, not 49. Untied arrivals still get the full drop, burst and stamp.
  • Target: one string to a random recruit among the 5 nearest with fewer than 3 strings. No hubs, no second ties.
  • Persistent: links live server-side next to recruits ({id, from, to}). A refresh or a new join never re-rolls them. A link only goes away when one of its ends leaves.
  • String physics: pin-to-pin line, redrawn every frame. Slack until 420px, then a weak spring (0.4 px/s² per 100px over). Untied baubles drift free, which keeps the crowd organic instead of settling into a grid.
  • Exits: when the oldest leaves, its strings retract and are not replaced.

PREFERS-REDUCED-MOTION

  • No drift, collisions or wobble. Baubles get a seeded scatter position that avoids the hero and QR.
  • Arrival: opacity 0→1 and scale .96→1, 200ms ease-out. No drop, squash or rings.
  • Strings fade in over 200ms instead of drawing. The stamp shows static for 1.5s.
  • Exit: the oldest fades out over 300ms. Ghost bauble on the empty board stays still.

COST AT 50 BAUBLES

  • One requestAnimationFrame loop. Write only transform per bauble (translate3d + rotate + scale), never left/top.
  • Box-shadows stay static. Never animate shadow, filter or clip-path. Ring bursts use border + transform + opacity only.
  • Strings: one absolutely positioned SVG with up to ~60 <line> elements, attributes updated in the same frame.
  • Collisions are 1,225 pair checks a frame, which is fine. No library needed.
  • Cork texture is a static background on the root. Drop-shadow filters only on the static hero and QR card.
  • Fallback if the presenter laptop chugs: drop the second shadow layer on baubles and turn off rotation wobble.
